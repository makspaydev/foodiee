Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Foodiee (foodiee.live) is a free recipe app for air-fryer and steamer cooking. This page explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the third-party services involved. We keep data collection to a minimum.

What we collect

  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4): when you visit, Google Analytics records anonymous usage data — pages viewed, approximate location (city/country), device and browser type, and how you arrived. This uses cookies. We use it only to understand which recipes are popular and how the site is used.
  • Your favourites & shopping list: stored only in your own browser (local storage). This never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • No accounts: Foodiee does not currently ask you to sign up, and we do not collect your name, email, or phone number.

Affiliate links (Amazon)

Some links to kitchen appliances are Amazon Associate links. As an Amazon Associate, Foodiee earns from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Clicking these links may set cookies controlled by Amazon under Amazon's privacy policy.

Swiggy Instamart ordering (coming soon)

We are building an optional feature to let you order recipe ingredients from Swiggy Instamart. When it launches:

  • You will connect your own Swiggy account through Swiggy's own secure login (OAuth). Foodiee never sees your Swiggy password or OTP.
  • We store only the access token for your session (encrypted), your chosen delivery address, and your shopping list — purely to build your cart.
  • You can disconnect at any time, which deletes your stored token. No order is ever placed without your explicit confirmation.
  • Order and transaction data is also governed by Swiggy's privacy policy.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies (above). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on. Foodiee works fine without analytics cookies.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — usage analytics
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate links
  • Swiggy Instamart — grocery ordering (when launched)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting

Your choices & contact

Since favourites and lists live only in your browser, you control them directly. For any privacy question, or to request deletion of data once Swiggy ordering is live, email shiva@foodiee.live.

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the "last updated" date above will reflect changes.
